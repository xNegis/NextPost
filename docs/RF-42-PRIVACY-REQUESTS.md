# Invited-beta privacy request procedure

`xnegis@gmail.com` is the monitored contact for privacy, support and beta feedback. This manual
procedure is the approved minimum before external invitations; it is not a substitute for RF-34 or
RF-35 when current contracts cannot satisfy a request safely.

1. Record the received time, requested right and a random internal case reference. Do not copy
   photographs, consultation text, credentials or private links into the case record.
2. Require the request from the account email and perform a separate authentication exchange. Never
   ask for or accept the account password, session cookie or Private-link token.
3. Resolve the account by the canonical account identifier. Bound every query/action to that account
   and use existing services/commands where available; do not improvise direct production edits.
4. For access/export, include only that person's account data, authored consultations and permitted
   participation outcomes. Exclude other voters, other accounts, credentials, operational secrets,
   storage keys and private capabilities. Deliver through an agreed secure channel, not an
   unprotected email attachment.
5. For erasure, explain current retained minimums and use the owning RF-34/RF-35 process. If it is
   not implemented or conflicts with RF-37/DEC-023 integrity, pause and groom the ticket rather than
   claiming completion.
6. Verify database access revocation and RF-37 physical-erasure convergence. Storage delay never
   reopens access. Do not mark physical completion while any exact S3 version remains retrievable.
7. Retain only case reference, request type, authentication/completion timestamps and outcome. Do
   not retain exported content or free-form copies as an audit archive.
8. Respond through `xnegis@gmail.com` with a plain-language outcome or a precise blocker.
